StockPing - Data Deletion Instructions

StockPing is an educational inventory notification and WhatsApp messaging project.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your personal information held by StockPing, send an email to:

harikeshan123@gmail.com

Use the subject: StockPing Data Deletion Request. Include the WhatsApp phone number associated with your request. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive credentials.

What Happens Next?

We will review your request, verify ownership where necessary, and delete applicable personal data under our control, subject to any necessary legal, security, or recordkeeping obligations.

Stop WhatsApp Notifications

You can send STOP to StockPing's WhatsApp number to opt out of notifications. Sending STOP does not automatically delete previously stored data.

Information Covered

Deletion requests may cover customer contact details, WhatsApp message records, and stock notification preferences associated with the requesting customer.
