# StockPing — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 8, 2026

## 1. Introduction

StockPing is an educational software project that demonstrates inventory management, stock availability notifications, and WhatsApp-based customer messaging.

The application is currently being developed and tested. It is not offered as a commercial service.

## 2. Information We Process

StockPing may process the following information during authorized testing:

- Customer phone numbers and WhatsApp identifiers.
- Incoming WhatsApp messages and outgoing replies.
- Product inquiries and stock availability requests.
- Waitlist subscriptions and notification preferences.
- Customer consent and opt-out records.
- Message delivery statuses, timestamps, and diagnostic information.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to process customer requests, respond to WhatsApp commands, check inventory availability, manage stock notification subscriptions, prevent duplicate processing, and troubleshoot the application.

## 4. WhatsApp Integration

StockPing integrates with the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Messages exchanged through WhatsApp are also subject to Meta's applicable privacy policies and terms.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

StockPing stores application records in its service databases during development and testing. Information is retained as needed for testing, troubleshooting, and maintaining application integrity, and may be removed when no longer required.

## 6. Customer Choices

Customers can send `STOP` to opt out of StockPing notifications. Opting out does not automatically delete historical records.

Customers may also request the deletion of information held by StockPing.
